# Technical Dense

Use compact but consistent rhythm, strong alignment, stable numeric and tabular presentation, explicit states, and fast interaction. Hierarchy comes from grouping, contrast, typography, and ordering rather than decorative scale.

Expose technical details only when they help the audience verify or act. Motion should preserve continuity and never delay repeat work.

Avoid tiny text, low-contrast chrome, monospace everywhere, terminal cosplay, card grids for tabular relationships, and reducing density merely to look modern.
