# Expressive Raw

Establish a coherent base, then break it deliberately. Use type as image, exposed boundaries, hard or tactile feedback, unconventional crops, and selective overlap when the product can support friction.

Every rupture needs a job: signal urgency, mark identity, separate a scene, or make an action physical. Maintain reading, focus, and touch clarity.

Avoid universal chaos, prime-number spacing, arbitrary rotation, absolute positioning as default, hostile forms, and copying neobrutalist borders or shadows without product meaning.
