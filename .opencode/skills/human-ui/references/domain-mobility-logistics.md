# Mobility and Logistics

Make origin, destination, current holder, live or stale state, ETA confidence, next event, contact, and recovery explicit. Status remains understandable without a map or color.

Support weak GPS, permissions, cancellation, rerouting, delay, offline use, and safe attention on mobile.

The domain does not require a fullscreen map, floating bottom card, large radius, moving vehicle icon, or constant animation.
