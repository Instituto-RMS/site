# Restrained Premium

Use controlled contrast, deliberate whitespace, precise typography, and a small number of high-quality details. Let content and alignment establish authority. Surfaces may be flat, bordered, softly elevated, or image-led according to the brand.

Motion is absent or functional: state transitions, focus, and continuity. A signature move may be a refined crop, unusual typographic scale, exact divider rhythm, or one material surface.

Avoid equating premium with beige, serif type, glass, large radius, sparse content, or slow animation. Preserve dense layouts when the audience needs density.
