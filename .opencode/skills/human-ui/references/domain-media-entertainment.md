# Media and Entertainment

Make title, creator, progress, active state, queue or sequence, availability, and rights context clear. Artwork may define atmosphere while controls retain stable contrast and placement.

Support missing artwork, playback errors, reduced bandwidth, captions or transcripts, keyboard control, and resume behavior.

The domain does not require dark mode, cover-derived accent colors, glow, equalizers, masonry, or fullscreen layouts.
