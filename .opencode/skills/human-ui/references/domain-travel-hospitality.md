# Travel and Hospitality

Expose location, dates, guests, availability, total price, policies, amenities, and uncertainty. Discovery may be emotional and image-led; booking must become precise and calm.

Support timezone, locale, cancellation, unavailable dates, map context, and weak media. Preserve search state across detail and checkout.

The domain does not require destination colors, serif headlines, pill controls, or cinematic motion. Those belong to chosen style and source material.
