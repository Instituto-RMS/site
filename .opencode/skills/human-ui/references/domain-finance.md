# Finance

Show currency, sign, units, periods, pending state, fees, and data freshness explicitly. Trend color follows business meaning, not numeric direction. Separate available, pending, projected, and historical values.

Support privacy, destructive confirmation, export/audit paths, and stable numeric alignment. Motion must not imply that settled values are fluctuating.

The domain does not require navy, green, dark mode, cards, or conservative geometry. Brand and audience decide aesthetics.
