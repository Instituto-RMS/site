# Pet Care

Distinguish the animal, owner, service or product, eligibility, scheduling, safety, and recurring care. Use warmth without obscuring medical, size, age, dosage, or appointment constraints.

Support multi-pet households, unavailable services, delivery or booking state, and trust signals.

The domain does not require cute illustration, pastel color, rounded geometry, playful motion, or photography. Audience and brand decide tone.
