# Composition DNA

## `perfect-space`

Build a full-scene canvas with a strong outer frame, centered promise, contextual pins, compact trust evidence, and a transaction bar anchored to the scene. Use when location or environment is the product. Keep pins sparse and meaningful.

## `image-shift`

Treat media as moving spatial objects rather than a fixed grid. Small images may drift, regroup, scale, or switch between gallery and list logic. Preserve orientation, selection, and reduced-motion fallback.

## `type-as-image`

Let typography carry identity through scale, crop, contrast, line breaks, or mixed roles. Protect reading order and responsive reflow. One expressive relationship is stronger than many font clashes.

## `dense-data`

Use alignment, compact rhythm, stable columns, semantic accents, and a clear drill-down path. Density comes from useful relationships, not smaller type. Keep exceptional items visually distinct from routine data.

## `asymmetric-auth`

Place a focused form beside a contextual scene, preview, or identity object without forcing a 50/50 split. Form contrast and progress stay stable; the visual side supplies motivation or consequence, not distraction.

## `editorial-closure`

End a page with a strong visual statement followed by a clearly organized footer. Use scale or imagery to create closure, then switch to calm utility. Avoid turning footer navigation into another hero.

## `product-stage`

Present one product, artwork, or feature as a staged object with generous context, restrained UI, and deliberate reveal. The object may rotate, replace, or expand, but actions and identity remain anchored.

## Blend rule

Choose one composition capsule as the signature move. A second may govern a separate region; do not merge every behavior into one screen.
