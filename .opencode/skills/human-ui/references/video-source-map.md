# Video Source Map

Maintenance evidence only. Observations come from extracted frames and short contact sheets; promoted DNA records interpretation separately.

## Admin panels

Sources: `admin-panel-1.mp4`, `admin-panel-2.mp4`.

- Stable left navigation and top-level period/action controls frame the work.
- Primary content is dense but calm: metrics, trend charts, alerts, and drill-down actions.
- Color is semantic and sparse; geometry stays regular rather than improvised.

Promoted: `dashboard`, `workspace`, `dense-data`, `technical-dense`.

## CTA

Source: `CTA-1.mp4`.

- One compact card explains a bonus before asking for action.
- Illustration overlaps the header region and establishes the focal point.
- Steps, benefit, and CTA form a direct vertical sequence.

Promoted: `guided-flow`, `friendly-soft`.

## Footers

Sources: `footer-1.avif`, `footer-2.avif`, `footer-4.avif`, `footer-5.avif`.

- A large image or narrative statement creates closure before utility links.
- Footer navigation returns to disciplined columns and quiet typography.
- Visual tone ranges from architectural photography to illustrated atmosphere; no shared brutalist requirement exists.

Promoted: `editorial-closure`, `landing-story`, `immersive-cinematic`.

## Liquid glass

Source: `liquid-glass.mp4`.

- Translucent action controls float over a dark live transit surface.
- Controls pick up blurred color from the route beneath while icons remain crisp.
- Glass is localized to interactive controls; the whole interface is not translucent.

Promoted: `liquid-glass`, `functional-feedback`, `live-tracking`.

## Mural

Source: `mural.mp4`.

- Saturated yellow field and compressed black display type create immediate identity.
- Composition uses a large text mass, small explanatory block, and compact media preview.
- Boundaries are direct and rectangular; warmth comes from message and color, not soft organic shapes.

Promoted: `expressive-raw`, `type-as-image`, `landing-story`.

## Neubrutalism

Sources: `neubrutalism-2.mp4`, `neubrutalism-line-slin.mp4`.

- One source uses saturated blocks, thick black borders, solid shadows, and oversized geometric type.
- The second uses an editorial/technical grid, mono metadata, serif display type, thin construction lines, and a restrained accent.
- Both expose structure, but only the first is canonical neobrutalism; the second is technical editorial.

Promoted: `neo-brutal`, `expressive-raw`, `technical-dense`, `type-as-image`.

## Perfect space

Source: `perfect-space.mp4`.

- Full-bleed cabin landscape is enclosed by a strong rounded frame.
- Amenity pins, map, rating, and copy orbit one spatial focal point.
- Booking fields form a single anchored transaction bar across the lower scene.

Promoted: `perfect-space`, `immersive-cinematic`, `travel-hospitality`, `guided-flow`.

## Pallet Ross

Source: `pallet-ross.mp4`.

- White stage uses extreme negative space around one central artwork.
- Headline and navigation stay restrained and stable while the artwork supplies character.
- Depth comes from the staged page surface and object, not heavy borders or brutalist shadows.

Promoted: `product-stage`, `restrained-premium`, `media-experience`.

## Shift photo

Source: `shift-photo.mp4`.

- Small media fragments occupy a large white spatial field instead of a conventional grid.
- Frames drift and regroup over time while navigation and view controls remain anchored.
- Gallery/list switching is explicit; motion is the organizational language.

Promoted: `image-shift`, `media-experience`, `weighted-spring`.

## Shopify Edition

Source: `shopify edition.mp4`.

- Opening is nearly black with sparse construction lines and small editorial type.
- A luminous object appears before the scene expands into full-bleed collage imagery.
- Motion progresses as cinematic chapters; the UI shell remains minimal.

Promoted: `product-stage`, `cinematic-scroll`, `immersive-cinematic`.

## Signup

Sources: `signup-1.avif`, `signup-3.mp4`, `signup-4.avif`, `signup-5.avif`.

- Focused dark form panel sits beside a contextual preview or atmospheric scene.
- Form content appears progressively while the visual anchor stays stable.
- Contrast, progress, and primary action remain clearer than the decorative side.

Promoted: `asymmetric-auth`, `guided-flow`, `atmospheric-depth`.

## Scroll animation

Source: `scrroll-animation-1.mp4`.

- Editorial typography establishes the opening before service and project regions enter.
- Contact-sheet frames show normal document progression rather than chaotic step animation.
- Device/project imagery appears later as narrative evidence; reading remains possible as a static page.

Promoted: `editorial-humanist`, `landing-story`, `cinematic-scroll` with restrained intensity.

## Typography

Sources: `typography-1.avif`, `typography-2.avif`, `typography-3.mp4`.

- Type alternates between refined technical editorial and bold monochrome display roles.
- Diagrams, halftone imagery, or restrained data visuals share alignment with text.
- Large headings carry identity while body and metadata remain readable and disciplined.

Promoted: `type-as-image`, `editorial-humanist`, `technical-dense`.

## Fora

- Centered community promise sits over an atmospheric landscape.
- Product UI preview is staged inside the environment rather than shown as a plain screenshot.
- Navigation and CTA remain restrained while depth comes from imagery and layered framing.
