# Surface DNA

Load only the capsule selected in the direction record.

## `warm-paper`

Use warm off-whites, restrained material variation, humanist type, and quiet boundaries to create tactility without nostalgia cosplay. Let texture support reading. Avoid mandatory grain, irregular radius, or sepia palettes.

## `liquid-glass`

Use translucent controls over changing content, with local blur, edge highlights, refraction-like color pickup, and strong foreground contrast. Glass must reveal context behind it and remain legible across light and dark regions. Avoid placing translucent layers over empty flat backgrounds or stacking glass everywhere.

## `neo-brutal`

Use high-contrast blocks, visible borders, solid offset depth, direct typography, and physical pressed states. Keep the grid legible; controlled misalignment is optional. Avoid confusing neobrutalism with universal chaos, hostility, or inaccessible forms.

## `atmospheric-depth`

Use imagery, tonal fields, environmental color, haze, or soft layered light to establish place. Foreground controls remain crisp. Depth may be cinematic, natural, or technical. Avoid generic gradient blobs and atmospheric effects unrelated to content.

## Blend rule

Select one dominant surface capsule. A second may appear only in one bounded region, such as glass controls over an atmospheric hero.
