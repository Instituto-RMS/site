# Landing Story

## Use / avoid

Use when sequencing ideas and emotion matters. Avoid as the shell for repeated operational work.

## Structural contract

Own narrative order, primary promise, proof, objection handling, and final action. Give each section a distinct job and visual rhythm. The opening must establish audience, value, and next action without depending on decoration.

## Required states

Handle slow or missing media, long copy, narrow screens, and anchored navigation. Keep CTA meaning stable throughout the page.

## Blend contract

As primary, own the page sequence. Let `search-discovery` supply a discovery module, `guided-flow` supply a conversion flow, and `media-experience` supply image behavior. Style controls atmosphere, not section purpose.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid interchangeable hero-copy-feature-pricing templates, repeated card grids, fake social proof, and motion that delays comprehension.
