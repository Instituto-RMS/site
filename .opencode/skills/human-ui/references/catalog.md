# Direction Catalog

Choose by interaction structure first, then visual style, domain constraints, and optional DNA.

## Product archetypes

- `landing-story`: persuasion, narrative sequencing, launch, portfolio, or branded presentation.
- `publication`: articles, news, knowledge, editorial feeds, or long-form reading.
- `dashboard`: metrics, monitoring, reporting, executive summaries, or analytical drill-down.
- `workspace`: repeated operational work, CRUD, editors, settings, productivity, or developer tools.
- `catalog`: products, menus, services, directories, or browsable inventories.
- `search-discovery`: queries, filters, comparison, recommendations, or exploratory browsing.
- `conversation`: human chat, support, communities, or AI assistants.
- `guided-flow`: onboarding, booking, checkout, application, upload, or multi-step setup.
- `live-tracking`: maps, ETA, delivery, dispatch, device state, or real-time progress.
- `media-experience`: galleries, libraries, players, albums, photography, or visual showcases.

## Styles

`restrained-premium`, `editorial-humanist`, `friendly-soft`, `technical-dense`, `immersive-cinematic`, `expressive-raw`.

## Domain overlays

`finance`, `health-medication`, `travel-hospitality`, `food-service`, `pet-care`, `mobility-logistics`, `media-entertainment`.

## Example blends

- Premium cabin booking: `landing-story + search-discovery + guided-flow`, `immersive-cinematic`, `travel-hospitality`, `product-stage`.
- B2B analytics: `dashboard + workspace`, `technical-dense` or `restrained-premium`, `dense-data`.
- Photography journal: `publication + media-experience`, `editorial-humanist`, `image-shift` or `type-as-image`.
- AI assistant: `conversation + workspace`, `restrained-premium`, `functional-feedback`.

Primary archetype owns the page. Secondary archetypes add bounded capabilities. Style never replaces product structure. Domain never dictates brand aesthetics.
