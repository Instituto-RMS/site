# Food Service

Show item identity, portions, price, availability, modifiers, allergens, timing, and fulfillment mode. Photography may lead but cannot replace labels and dietary information.

Keep cart changes and totals visible. Handle sold-out items, substitutions, scheduled orders, and location constraints.

The domain does not require warm colors, rounded cards, hover zoom, or photography when the actual brand uses illustration or typography.
