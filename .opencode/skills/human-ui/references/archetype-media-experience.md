# Media Experience

## Structural contract

Own media hierarchy, framing, selection, metadata, and continuity between browsing and detail or playback. UI contrast and geometry should support the work rather than impose a default aesthetic.

## Required states

Cover missing media, loading, alternate aspect ratios, captions, keyboard navigation, fullscreen or lightbox exit, playback errors, and reduced bandwidth. Preserve controls without permanently covering content.

## Blend contract

Use `publication` for authored context, `landing-story` for presentation, `search-discovery` for libraries, and `catalog` when media is purchased or booked.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid automatic dark mode, decorative surfaces behind every image, forced masonry, hidden essential controls, and image crops that destroy subject or composition.
