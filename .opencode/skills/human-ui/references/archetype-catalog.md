# Catalog

## Structural contract

Own category orientation, item comparison, availability, price or commitment, and the transition to detail or action. Information density must match decision complexity.

## Required states

Cover unavailable items, variants, missing media, long names, promotions, loading, empty filters, and cart or selection feedback. Product imagery may lead, but must not hide critical decision data.

## Blend contract

Use `search-discovery` for large inventories, `guided-flow` for purchase or booking, and `media-experience` when visual evaluation dominates. Domain overlays define safety and vocabulary.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid identical cards for incomparable items, badge clutter, hidden total cost, image-only choices without labels, and treating checkout as a decorative continuation of browsing.
