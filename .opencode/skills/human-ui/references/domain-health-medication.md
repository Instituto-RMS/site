# Health and Medication

Make dosage, timing, warnings, identity, appointment state, and next action unambiguous. Use plain language and redundant cues for critical status. Account for stress, low vision, motor limitations, and older audiences.

Protect sensitive data and distinguish clinical guidance from general content. Errors must explain safe recovery.

The domain does not mandate blue/green palettes, rounded geometry, spacious layouts, or zero illustration. Safety and audience determine constraints.
