# Friendly Soft

Use clear language, forgiving spacing, legible controls, warm feedback, and shapes consistent with the brand. Softness may come from tone, color relationships, illustration, motion, or geometry; rounded cards are not mandatory.

Make success and recovery reassuring. Use playful details where they reinforce content or action, not across every component.

Avoid childish copy, oversized controls without need, pastel-by-default palettes, bounce on serious actions, and hiding complex decisions behind friendliness.
